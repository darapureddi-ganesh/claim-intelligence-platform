STANDARD HEALTH INSURANCE POLICY WORDING
Policy Reference: HC-STD-2024
Insurer: Pilot General Insurance Co. Ltd.
IRDAI Registration No: XXXX

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Insured" means the person(s) named in the Schedule.
1.2 "Sum Insured" means the amount shown in the Schedule, being the maximum liability of the Insurer.
1.3 "Hospitalization" means admission to a Hospital for a minimum period of 24 consecutive hours.
1.4 "Pre-existing Disease" means any condition, ailment or injury for which the Insured had signs, symptoms, was diagnosed, or received medical advice/treatment within 48 months prior to the inception of this policy.

2. COVERAGE
2.1 The Insurer agrees to pay for hospitalization expenses including room charges, nursing charges, surgeon fees, and pharmacy costs incurred by the Insured during the Policy Period.
2.2 Emergency medical treatment is covered up to the Sum Insured.
2.3 Day care procedures listed in Annexure A are covered subject to the limits specified therein.
2.4 Maternity benefits are payable after 9 months of continuous coverage, up to INR 50,000 per policy year.

3. EXCLUSIONS
3.1 This policy does not cover any Pre-existing Disease for the first 48 months of continuous coverage.
3.2 Cosmetic or aesthetic treatment is excluded unless necessitated by an Accident.
3.3 Treatment for self-inflicted injuries or attempted suicide is excluded.
3.4 OPD (outpatient) consultations are excluded unless specifically endorsed.
3.5 Waiting period: Claims arising within 30 days of policy inception are excluded except for Accidents.

4. CONDITIONS
4.1 All claims must be intimated to the Insurer within 7 days of hospitalization or event giving rise to the claim.
4.2 Pre-authorization is required for planned hospitalization exceeding INR 50,000.
4.3 All original bills, receipts, and discharge summaries must be submitted within 30 days of discharge.
4.4 Provided that the Insurer reserves the right to appoint an Independent Medical Examiner whose findings shall be final.

5. HIGH-VALUE CLAIMS
5.1 Claims exceeding INR 5,00,000 must be reviewed by a Senior Claims Adjuster.
5.2 Claims with a complexity score above threshold shall be referred to the Senior Adjuster queue for detailed assessment.
5.3 Claims flagged for potential fraud investigation shall be routed to the Fraud Investigation Unit prior to settlement.

6. ENDORSEMENTS
6.1 Critical Illness Rider (if applicable): Pays a lump sum on diagnosis of listed critical illnesses including cancer, stroke, kidney failure. Waiting period: 90 days.
6.2 Hospital Daily Cash Benefit: Pays INR 500 per day of hospitalization up to 30 days per policy year.

MOTOR INSURANCE SECTION (Third Party + Own Damage)

7. MOTOR COVERAGE
7.1 Third party liability is covered as per the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988.
7.2 Own damage to the insured vehicle is covered subject to the Insured Declared Value (IDV).
7.3 Total loss is declared when repair cost exceeds 75% of IDV.
7.4 Claims arising from collision, accident, or natural calamity are covered.

8. MOTOR EXCLUSIONS
8.1 Damage due to driving under influence of alcohol or drugs is excluded.
8.2 Depreciation on consumable parts is excluded.
8.3 Mechanical or electrical breakdown not caused by accident is excluded.

PROPERTY INSURANCE SECTION

9. PROPERTY COVERAGE
9.1 Loss or damage to building and contents caused by fire, lightning, explosion, flood, and allied perils is covered.
9.2 Theft and burglary are covered subject to first loss deductible.
9.3 Business interruption losses may be covered under separate endorsement.

10. PROPERTY EXCLUSIONS
10.1 War, nuclear, and terrorism risks are excluded.
10.2 Gradual deterioration, wear and tear are excluded.
10.3 Damage caused by earth movement is excluded unless specifically endorsed.
